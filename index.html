04 // LEADERSHIP & IMPACT

Leadership & Field Experiences

Disability Awareness & Experiential Carnival

Lead Organizer & Simulation Designer · Boon Lay CC (50 Hours VIA)
National-Level Community Impact

As an organizer for the national-level Disability Awareness and Experiential Carnival ("Beyond the Limits") at Boon Lay Community Club, I spearheaded the design of interactive, hands-on simulations that moved beyond passive awareness to foster genuine empathy, understanding, and inclusivity.

My team developed experiential challenges—including blindfolded obstacle navigation, non-verbal communication stations, and wheelchair soccer—enabling participants to directly encounter and appreciate the physical and emotional barriers faced daily by disabled individuals. Meticulously oversaw logistics, manpower planning, and inter-agency coordination with Boon Lay CC and voluntary welfare organizations.

BorgWarner Engineering Internship

MOE ETPS Work Attachment Programme · BorgWarner Inc.
Corporate Engineering Exposure

Completed an engineering work attachment with global automotive and e-mobility technology leader BorgWarner Inc. under the MOE Engineering & Technology Programme. Collaborated within multidisciplinary engineering teams, observing and evaluating manufacturing and diagnostic protocols.

Developed essential 21st-century workplace competencies, including active listening, root-cause process analysis, and inter-departmental communication. Gained direct appreciation for how technical precision, reliability, and human collaboration interface in production systems.

Reading Wonders Literacy Programme

Primary 1 Student Reading Mentor · SG CARES @ Boon Lay / West Grove Primary School
Educational Equity VIA

Guided by a commitment to educational equal opportunity, led literacy intervention sessions for Primary 1 pupils facing reading and comprehension hurdles.

Adopted a flexible, student-centered approach—incorporating contextual storytelling and visual prompts matched to each child's pace. Learned that celebrating small wins builds genuine self-agency and academic confidence.

Project Reminiscence (Elderly Outreach)
